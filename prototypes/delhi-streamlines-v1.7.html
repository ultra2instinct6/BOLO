CONTROLS

PS5-style browser layout*
Left stick ride / carve · R2 push or pump the bowl · hold/release Cross ollie · Square flip · Triangle grab · R1 grind (brief early press works) · L1 manual · L2 revert · Circle brake · L3 Instinct · Create save spot · R3 retry · Options city atlas. Flow adds the right-stick flick. D-pad selects a fast-travel neighborhood in the menu.

Keyboard + mouse
WASD / arrows ride or carve · Shift push / pump · hold/release Space or left click pop · J flip · K grab · E grind · M manual · Q revert · B brake · I Instinct · C save spot · G ghost · R retry · Tab atlas. Mouse wheel zoom; Z/X correct manual balance.

Mobile
Left thumb rides or carves a bowl; hold PUSH while descending to pump, and hold/release POP to ollie. INSTINCT reveals routes, SAVE places your BOLO Spot, MAP opens the city atlas. Rotate for more room. Choose reduced motion or low detail when preferred.

*A browser prototype using the Standard Gamepad layout, not a native PS5 build. The city and rideable connectors are fictional. Monument grounds and working shops remain protected scenery; word pronunciations are concept copy pending local review.
