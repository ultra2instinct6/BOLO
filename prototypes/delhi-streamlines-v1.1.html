Find your line through Delhi.

A fictional Delhi district with a Punjabi neighborhood, market lanes, a marked crossing, metro plaza, bazaar square and a community skate ground. Follow the glowing spot markers, then build your own line.

PS5 controller in a supported browser: left stick / D-pad steer, R2 push, Cross pop, Square flip, Triangle grab, R1 grind, L1 manual, Circle brake, Create set spot, R3 retry, Options pause. Browser mappings vary.

Respect the street: skate features are marked. Shops and stalls are working spaces; traffic stops at the crossing when you enter it. Move to the skate ground for uninterrupted bowl, bank and rail lines.
