CONTROLS

PS5 controller*
Left stick steer · R2 push · hold/release Cross ollie · Square flip · Triangle grab · R1 grind · L1 manual · L2 revert · Circle brake · L3 Instinct · Create save spot / start ghost recording · R3 retry · Options map. In Flow mode, flick right stick down then up/diagonal to pop or flip; spin in air with right-stick X. D-pad selects a district in the menu.

Keyboard + mouse
WASD or arrows steer · Shift push · hold/release Space or left click pop · J / middle click flip · K / right click grab · E grind · M manual · Q revert · B brake · I Instinct · C save spot · G replay ghost · R retry · Tab map. Mouse wheel zoom; Z/X correct manual balance.

Mobile
Left thumb steers; hold PUSH. Hold and release POP. FLIP or GRAB in air, GRIND near a teal rail, hold MANUAL to connect a line, REVERT on landing. INSTINCT reveals discoveries. SAVE records a five-second ghost; MAP opens replay and Line Run. Tap ♫ to mute.

*Standard Gamepad support depends on the browser and controller. Native DualSense features need a console build. Landmarks are scenery; marked public skate areas are fictional. Audio starts after a user gesture and can be muted. Your save stays in this browser unless you export it.
